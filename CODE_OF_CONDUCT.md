# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text at that link applies; the summary below does not replace it.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and empathetic; give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject contributions, or ban contributors, for behavior they deem inappropriate. This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

Report unacceptable behavior to hello@planarhq.com (planned address). All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is respected. Enforcement follows the guidelines in the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
